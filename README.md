## What I do

I build products end to end — the API, the database, the mobile client, the web
client and the deploy pipeline. Not as a claim about breadth; it is just how I
work best. When one person holds the API contract, the mobile app and the
release process in their head at once, most of the usual friction stops
existing, and an idea can get to the App Store without a team around it first.

I use AI heavily and would rather say so plainly. It earns its place in
scaffolding, archaeology through undocumented formats and second opinions at one
in the morning. It does not decide what to build, and it does not excuse me from
understanding what shipped.

## Previously

platform: workout tracking, a video feed, a 1.7M-food nutrition database, a
per-muscle recovery model, an Apple Watch app. I owned the whole stack. It is
shut down, and I keep it on the record because it was too much surface area for
the team behind it — a lesson about scope I would rather have learned on my own
project. YokedCache and the Expo Media Engine came out of it and outlived it.
